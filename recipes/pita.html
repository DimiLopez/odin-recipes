Pita

Description

Traditional pita bread, freshly baked until golden and lightly crisp

on the outside while staying soft and fluffy inside. Perfect for

dipping, filling, or enjoying alongside your favorite dishes.

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 150ml lukewarm water
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 5g dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 30 ml olive oil for kneading
  • 8 tsp olive oil for baking
