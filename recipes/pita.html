Pita

Description

Traditional pita bread, freshly baked until golden and lightly crisp

on the outside while staying soft and fluffy inside. Perfect for

dipping, filling, or enjoying alongside your favorite dishes.

Ingredients

  • 250g flour
  • 150ml lukewarm water
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 5g dry yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 30 ml olive oil for kneading
  • 8 tsp olive oil for baking

Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, dry yeast, and sugar in a bowl.
  2. Add lukewarm water and knead until the dough is no longer sticky.
  3. Gradually work in the olive oil with your hands.
  4. Cover and let the dough rest in a warm place for 30 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into 4 equal pieces.
  6. Roll each piece out to 2–3 mm thick. Sprinkle with a little flour if needed.
  7. Heat a pan and brush each pita with 1 tsp olive oil on both sides.
  8. Cook each pita over medium heat for about 4 minutes per side.
