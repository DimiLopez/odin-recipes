Tzatziki

Description

Tzatziki is a refreshing Greek sauce made with creamy yogurt, cucumber, garlic, and herbs.

It is often served as a dip or alongside grilled meat and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Greek yogurt
  • 1 cucumber
  • 4 cloves of garlic
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Stir the Greek yogurt thoroughly.
  2. Peel the cucumber and grate it into fine julienne strips.
  3. Sprinkle salt over the cucumber and wait until the cucumber juice settles.
  4. In the meantime, peel the garlic gloves and press them into the yogurt.
  5. Put the cucumber in a clean cloth and squeeze out the juice.
  6. Add the dry cucumber to the yogurt and stir it in.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
